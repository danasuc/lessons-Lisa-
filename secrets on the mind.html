2. Reading: The State of Flow

Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi devoted his life to studying happiness. When he was a boy, he noticed that although many adults were wealthy, they were not always happy. This sometimes led them to behave in unpredictable ways. As a result of this, he decided to carry out extensive research to understand what happiness actually is.

He expanded his study by inventing the "experience sampling method." Ordinary people were asked to keep an electronic pager for a week, which gave out a beeping sound eight times a day. Every time it did so, they wrote down exactly how they felt and how much they were concentrating. This allowed researchers to track their mood objectively.

He discovered that people who achieve an ambition are not necessarily those with high incomes. In fact, people are happiest when they are in a state of "flow" — a zone of intense concentration where a challenge perfectly matches their skill level. This proves that achieving true satisfaction has very little to do with how much money we make.

3. Text Discussion

  • 1. In your opinion, why did the author use pagers ("experience sampling") instead of just asking people "Are you happy?" at the end of the day?
  • 2. The text states that "achieving true satisfaction has very little to do with how much money we make." Do you agree with this statement in the context of modern society?
  • 3. Can studying at school or preparing for the EGE trigger a "flow state," or does it only happen with hobbies?

6. Speaking: EGE Task 4

Imagine you are doing a project "Finding Focus" with your friend.

You have found two photos for the project: Photo 1 shows a girl reading a book in a quiet park. Photo 2 shows teenagers playing a loud, active video game together in a dark room. Leave a voice message to your friend.

In your 3-minute voice message, you must:

  • Briefly describe the photos, noting the differences (quiet solo activity vs. loud group activity).
  • Explain how these activities illustrate the project "Finding Focus".
  • State the advantages and disadvantages of both ways of achieving a flow state.
  • Express your opinion on the subject of the project: which way of focusing do you prefer and why?

Useful phrases to use:

• "The first photo depicts... whereas the second one illustrates..."

• "The girl must be deeply immersed in her book, while the gamers seem to be..."

• "A clear advantage of the first activity is... However, a major drawback is..."
